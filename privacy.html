Privacy Policy

Hidden Camera Finder: SpyCam · Effective 8 November 2026

Short version: the app collects nothing. Everything it finds stays on your iPhone.

What the app does on your device

  • Local network scan. With your permission, the app looks for devices on the Wi-Fi network you are joined to (IP addresses, open ports, device names and the titles of their web pages) to identify cameras and microphones.
  • Camera. The lens-glint and infrared checks use your camera live. Images are analysed on the device and are never saved or sent anywhere.
  • Saved results. Scan history and any nicknames you give devices are stored only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. It does not access your location, and it does not send scan results or any other data to us or anyone else.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
