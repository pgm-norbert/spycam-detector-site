Privacy Policy

Hidden Camera Finder: SpyCam · iPhone and Android · Effective 22 November 2026

Short version: the app collects nothing. Everything it finds stays on your phone.

This policy covers SpyCam on iPhone (App Store) and on Android (Google Play). Both versions work the same way.

What the app does on your device

  • Local network scan. The app looks for devices on the Wi-Fi network you are joined to (IP addresses, open ports, device names and the titles of their web pages) to identify cameras and microphones. On iPhone it asks for Local Network permission first.
  • Camera. The room sweep uses your camera and flashlight live. Images are analysed on the device and are never saved or sent anywhere. The app asks for camera permission only when you open the sweep.
  • Saved results. Scan history and any nicknames you give devices are stored only on your phone, and are not included in cloud backups on Android. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Evidence report. If you create a PDF report, it is saved on your phone and only leaves it if you choose to share it.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not ask for or access your location, and it does not send scan results or any other data to us or anyone else.

Purchases

SpyCam Pro is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details. The app only asks the store whether Pro has been bought on your account. Apple's and Google's own privacy policies cover the purchase.

Your rights and deleting data

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. To remove everything the app stored, delete the app (or on Android, use Settings → Apps → SpyCam → Storage → Clear storage).

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
